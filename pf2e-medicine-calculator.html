Rules assumptions

  • Rules baseline is Pathfinder 2e Remaster for Treat Wounds, Risky Surgery, and Battle Medicine.
  • DC tiers in scope are 15/20/30/40 with proficiency gating (Trained/Expert/Master/Legendary).
  • Typed bonuses (circumstance, item, status) use highest value only; untyped bonuses stack.
  • Risky Surgery adds +2 circumstance to Treat Wounds and upgrades success to critical success.
  • Battle Medicine availability requires the Battle Medicine feat toggle.
  • Cooldown, target count, and immunity changes are informational notes in output/macros.
